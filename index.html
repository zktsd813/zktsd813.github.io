About

I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at KAIST, advised by Prof. Jaehyuk Huh at the Computer Architecture and Systems Lab (CASYS). My research focuses on system software for memory systems, particularly CXL-based memory pooling and tiered memory. I am also building infrastructure for LLM agents, with a focus on scalable and resource-efficient agent sandboxes.
